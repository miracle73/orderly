User guide

Go from sign-up to taking paid phone orders in about 5 minutes.

1. Create your account

  1. Click Get started and enter your business name, email and a password (8+ characters).
  2. Open the Verify your email message and click the button. Check Spam if you don't see it.
  3. You land on your dashboard, and a welcome email arrives.

2. Add your menu

  1. Open the Menu tab.
  2. Enter the item name, other names customers use (e.g. "jollof" for Jollof Rice), and the price in naira, then click Add item.
  3. To edit, change the name, other names or price directly in the table; it saves when you click away. Untick Available when an item sells out. Use Delete to remove it.
The AI only sells what's on your menu, at exactly these prices.

3. Set up payments

  1. Open Setup → Get paid.
  2. Choose your bank, enter your 10-digit account number, and click Verify & save.
  3. Check the account name shown is yours. Payments now settle into this account. You'll get an email whenever it changes.

4. Share your number and link

  • Phone: customers call the Orderly number shown on Setup and say your business name or your 4-digit code.
  • Web: under Your customer ordering page, click Copy link and share it on WhatsApp, Instagram and your flyers. That page is exactly what your customers see.

5. Handle orders

  1. New orders appear on the Orders tab within seconds, and you get an email.
  2. Awaiting payment means the link was sent. Paid means the money is confirmed; start preparing.
  3. Use the Kitchen dropdown to move a paid order from new to preparing, ready and delivered.
  4. Today's paid orders and revenue are at the top.

6. Bookings

On the Bookings tab, add a customer, service, date and price. Change the status to completed when done; completed bookings count as income.

7. Money (cashflow and accounts)

The Money tab shows money in, money out and profit for 7, 30 or 90 days or 12 months, with a daily cashflow chart and expenses by category. Paid orders and completed bookings are added automatically; record other income and expenses (rent, stock, salaries) yourself.

8. Receipts

On the Orders tab, click Receipt next to any paid order to open a printable receipt. Print it, save it as a PDF, or share the link on WhatsApp.

9. Review calls

The Calls tab lists every conversation with its transcript, and recordings for phone calls.

10. Your account

  • Change password: Setup → Account security.
  • Forgot password: on the login page, click Forgot password? and follow the emailed link (valid for 1 hour).
  • Rename your business: Setup → Business.
  • Sign in with Google: use "Continue with Google" on the login page.
  • Use Orderly from Claude or ChatGPT: Setup → Connect AI assistants (MCP) → Create MCP key, then add the server URL and key in your assistant. Ask things like "How much did I make today?" or "Mark order 12 ready".
