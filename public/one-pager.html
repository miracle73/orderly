Orderly

An AI assistant and back office for small and medium businesses across Africa. It takes orders and bookings, collects payment through Paystack, and keeps the books: orders, cashflow, accounting and receipts.

The problem

Small and medium businesses across Africa (food vendors, salons, shops, clinics, service providers) lose sales every day to unanswered calls. The phone rings during the lunch rush, nobody picks up, and the customer orders elsewhere. Hiring someone to answer the phone full time isn't affordable for most of them.

The solution

📞 Answers every call

One shared line serves every business. Callers say the business name or its 4-digit code and are put straight through.

🧠 Takes the order naturally

Understands "make that two", Pidgin and changes of mind, reads the order back, and only proceeds on a clear yes.

💳 Collects payment

Sends a Paystack link. The money settles into the business's own bank account through a Paystack subaccount.

📋 Live kitchen board

Paid orders appear on the dashboard and move from new to preparing, ready and delivered. Email alerts for every order.

Why it's safe

The AI only talks. A separate, deterministic order system holds the basket and checks every change through a validated tool call. Prices come from the business's menu, never from the AI, and a payment counts only when its amount and reference match exactly. A confused turn of conversation can't corrupt an order or charge someone the wrong amount.

How it works

  1. Customer calls the shared line, or opens the business's ordering page.
  2. The AI takes the order and confirms it.
  3. Paystack link → customer pays → the order is marked Paid and the business gets an email.
  4. The kitchen prepares it and updates its status.

Any small or medium business in Africa that sells by phone, WhatsApp or walk-in: restaurants, shops, salons, pharmacies, laundries, clinics and service providers. They get an AI assistant plus simple bookings, cashflow and receipts, with no accountant or support team needed.
