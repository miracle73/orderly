Frequently asked questions

For businesses

Who is Orderly for?

Small and medium businesses across Africa, not just food: shops, salons, pharmacies, laundries, clinics and service providers. Payments currently run in Nigerian naira through Paystack.

Can I track bookings, cashflow and receipts?

Yes. The Bookings tab manages appointments, and the Money tab shows money in, money out and profit (paid orders and completed bookings are added automatically). Every paid order has a Receipt you can print or share on WhatsApp.

What is Orderly?

An AI assistant and back office for your business. It answers phone calls, takes orders, confirms them, and sends the customer a payment link. You see every order on your dashboard.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Share your customer ordering page (Setup → Your customer ordering page) on WhatsApp, Instagram and flyers. Customers chat with your AI assistant there to order and pay.

How do I get paid?

Add your bank and account number on the Setup page. Customers pay through Paystack by card, transfer or USSD, and Paystack settles the money into your bank account on its normal payout schedule. You never need Paystack keys or technical setup.

Can the AI charge a customer the wrong price?

No. The AI never sets prices. They come from your menu, and every change to an order is checked by a separate system. A payment only counts if the amount matches the order exactly.

What if a customer changes their mind?

That's expected. "Actually, make that two" updates the order. If they change something after confirming, the AI reads the new order back and confirms again before taking payment.

Does it understand Pidgin?

Yes. It's designed for Nigerian English and understands common Pidgin, like "abeg" or "make e be two". It says prices the Nigerian way, e.g. "two thousand, five hundred naira".

How do I know when an order comes in?

It appears on your dashboard within seconds, and you get an email when an order is confirmed and again when it's paid.

Can I change my menu or prices?

Any time, on the Menu tab. Changes take effect on the next call. Untick "Available" to stop selling an item that's sold out.

Can I listen to calls?

Yes. The Calls tab has the transcript of every conversation, plus recordings for phone calls.

I forgot my password.

Click "Forgot password?" on the login page and we'll email you a reset link.

For customers

How do I order?

Call the Orderly number, say the business name or code, then tell the assistant what you want. Or open the business's ordering link and chat.

How do I pay?

After you confirm, you get a Paystack payment link. Pay by card, bank transfer or USSD. The business is notified immediately.

Is my payment secure?

Payments are handled entirely by Paystack. Orderly never sees your card details.
